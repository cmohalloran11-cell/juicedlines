Privacy Policy

Last updated: · Effective for the free public beta
Plain-language summary: JUICED doesn't sell your data. We collect the minimum needed to run your account (email, and whatever you save — watchlists, portfolio entries, alerts). If you use it as a guest, that data stays in your own browser and never reaches our servers. We don't currently process any payment information because JUICED doesn't have a paid tier yet.

1. What we collect

If you're signed inWhat it is
Account infoEmail address and display name, from your chosen sign-in method (Google OAuth or email/password via Supabase). We don't set or see your password directly — that's handled by Supabase.
WatchlistsProps you've saved to track.
Portfolio entriesPlays you've logged (line, side, stake, odds, result) for your own tracking — this is self-reported by you, not a real wager placed through us.
AlertsThe metric/threshold rules you've configured (e.g. "notify when Juice Score ≥ 80").
If you're a guest (not signed in)What it is
Local watchlist/portfolio/notesStored only in your browser's local storage. It never reaches our servers and is lost if you clear browser data or switch devices.

2. What we don't collect

We do not collect payment or billing information of any kind — JUICED is currently free and has no paid tier or checkout flow. We do not request or store government ID, SSN, or other sensitive identity documents. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

3. Automatically collected technical data

Like most web services, our server logs basic request metadata (IP address, timestamp, requested path, response status) for operating and securing the Service — for example, detecting abuse and diagnosing errors. If error-monitoring is enabled on a given deployment, a crash report may include the request path and a stack trace, but is configured to avoid capturing full request bodies or credentials.

4. Third parties we rely on

ServiceWhy
SupabaseAuthentication (sign-in) and, depending on deployment, the account database.
MLB Stats API, ESPNPublic sports data used to build projections. We don't send them any of your personal data.
PrizePicks, Underdog, SleeperPublic prop-line data we display for comparison. We are not affiliated with them and don't share your account data with them.
Google Gemini or Anthropic (optional, "AI Juice" feature)If enabled on a deployment, generates a plain-language explanation of a projection. Only the specific projection's own numbers are sent — never your account info.
Xweather (optional)Weather conditions for outdoor games. No personal data involved.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use browser local/session storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences (like your watchlist, as a guest, or UI settings like sidebar state). We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time. Deleting your account removes your stored watchlists, portfolio entries, and alerts. Guest data is entirely in your control already — clearing your browser's local storage removes it immediately, no request needed.

7. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. Some operational data (like line-movement history) is retained on a rolling window and pruned automatically after a bounded period, independent of any individual account.

8. Children's privacy

JUICED is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under the legal age for sports wagering content in their jurisdiction. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone underage.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service evolves — particularly if a paid tier is introduced in the future, which would add a payment processor to the list above and be reflected here before it happens. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy, or a request to access/delete your data, can be sent through the feedback option available in the app once signed in.

This page is a good-faith baseline appropriate for a free informational beta with no payment processing. It is not a substitute for review by a qualified attorney, particularly once the Service accepts payment or expands its jurisdictional footprint.
